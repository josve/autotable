Instructions

Drag mouse to move or select
Press F or right-click to flip or rotate
Press R to rotate backwards; for example hide your hand
Hold SPACE (or press Q) to look down
Hold Z to zoom in
Hold X to zoom out
Press P to toggle perspective mode

On touch screens:
Drag to move or select
Tap to select, double tap to flip
Use the buttons at the bottom to flip, look down and zoom, and ☰ to hide this menu
